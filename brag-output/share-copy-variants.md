# Hackathon submission (under 120 words)

Deaf-Talks (SignStories) is a platform for learning South African Sign Language through Deaf storytellers’ stories. Storytellers upload short videos with English captions, tag key signs, and control consent, usage and credit. Learners browse published stories, watch with captions and credit, and jump to a sign’s timestamp. Private storage, row-level security and five-minute playback links support those boundaries. Built with Next.js, Supabase, Vercel and Codex for the OpenAI × Andela hackathon. Next: practice recordings, human teacher feedback and storyteller-approved, text-only AI caption assistance. No AI judges signing.

Live: https://deaf-talks.vercel.app
Repository: https://github.com/tinothecreator/Deaf-Talks
